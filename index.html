What to fix, in order

Make calls and site visits one tap awayAdd "Book a site visit" / "Call me" buttons to the first answer, invite every qualified lead, and track visits and calls agreed.
Fix the handoverGive agents the bot's summary, drop the generic opener, route high-intent chats immediately, and call instead of messaging again.
Stop re-askingThe bot asks again for budget or configuration already given in 8% of leads.
Handle voice notes20 of the 68 unanswered Replai chats ended on an audio message.
Cover eveningsAn evening enquiry waits about 10 h for a person on both systems; let the bot book the call or visit.
Log cached tokensTurns the cost range into one real number.

Read with care

  • Replai is young: 782 ad enquiries over 3 months, still ramping (143 → 290 → 420 a month).
  • Agents join Replai chats only after the customer has gone quiet, so part of the 11% reply gap is selection.
  • The quality review checks consistency within a chat, not prices against Marathon's price sheet. Each system's style is recognisable, so it isn't fully blind.
  • No outcome data yet. Site visits and bookings need Marathon's CRM; cost per lead needs the Vati invoice and agent cost.
  • Inbound = the customer wrote first, with no message from Marathon in the previous 72 h; 24 h of silence starts a new conversation.
